INVESTMENTS (requested)
An investment event is a single EXECUTED TRADE that changes how many shares/units you hold: a purchase, a disposal, an RSU/award vest, a sell-to-cover, a transfer of shares between accounts, or a stock split.

Extract these ONLY from executed-trade / disposal / vesting tables — rows that have an execution date, a quantity of shares, and a price per share. A statement usually contains only a handful of these. Do NOT invent one event per holding.

A single document may contain several separate trade tables — for example one per account or section (such as an "Invest account" and a "Stocks ISA account"), or per-symbol blocks. Scan the ENTIRE document and extract the qualifying trades from every such table, not just the first one.

Do NOT emit an investment event for any of the following (they are not share trades):
- Dividends or interest. These are income paid in cash, not a change in share count. A "dividend" label or a "gross amount per share" column is the tell — ignore these rows entirely.
- Open positions / holdings summaries. Those describe what you hold at a point in time, not a trade.
- Fees, taxes, deposits, withdrawals, or other cash movements.

Each entry has this shape:
```
{
  "event_type": "buy",
  "symbol": "VUSA",
  "date": "2026-03-15T00:00:00",
  "quantity": "10",
  "price_per_share": "76.32",
  "total_value": "763.20",
  "fee": "0",
  "currency": "GBP",
  "notes": null,
  "row_confidence": 0.97
}
```

`event_type` is one of: `buy`, `sell`, `vest`, `withhold`, `transfer`, `split`.
- `buy` / `sell`: open-market purchase / disposal for cash.
- `vest`: RSU/award shares released to you. quantity = the GROSS shares released
  (the full vested amount BEFORE tax withholding), never the net/disbursed amount.
- `withhold`: the shares sold or withheld to cover tax at that vest (the
  "sold to cover" portion).
- `transfer`: shares moved into or out of the account (not a disposal).
- `split`: stock-split adjustment.

CRITICAL for RSU / SAR / equity-compensation releases: a single release produces
TWO events — a `vest` for the GROSS shares released and a `withhold` for the shares
sold to cover tax. The shares you keep = vest minus withhold. When the statement
lists "released"/"vested", "sold to cover"/"withheld", and "disbursed"/"net", set
vest = released (gross) and withhold = sold-to-cover. NEVER set the `vest` quantity
to the net/disbursed shares while also recording the withhold: that double-counts
the tax shares, corrupts the cost basis, and drives the CGT disposal pool negative.
A column labelled "Disbursed", "Net shares", or "Shares issued" shows the post-tax
(net) amount and must not be used as the vest quantity; the vest quantity is always
the larger gross "Released"/"Vested" figure. Also capture the per-share Release Price
or FMV for every vest: do not emit a vest with a price of 0 when the statement shows a
release price for that event.

Field meanings:
- `date`: the execution date/time of the trade.
- `quantity`: the number of shares/units in this single trade (the position size of the disposal or purchase).
- `price_per_share`: the execution price per share, in the instrument's native currency (use the EXECUTION PRICE column, not an average price or FX rate).
- `fee`: broker / exchange / government fees on the trade. Use `"0"` only if the statement genuinely shows none.

Dates use ISO 8601 datetime (`YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS`); use `T00:00:00` if only the date is given.
