HOLDINGS PERIOD: quarterly
For every calendar quarter covered by the documents, emit an end-of-quarter snapshot per distinct holding (`as_of` = last day of the quarter: 31 Mar, 30 Jun, 30 Sep, 31 Dec).

Anchor to labelled balances first. When a statement prints an authoritative balance line ("Opening Balance", "Closing Balance", "Balance brought/carried forward", "New Balance"), pin it as a `derived: false` quarter-end anchor (the opening of a quarter equals the prior quarter's close), and only DERIVE the quarter-ends that have no labelled balance by working through transactions between anchors. A labelled balance beats summing transactions; never overwrite it with a derived value.

If a quarter has no labelled balance, work forwards or backwards using the transactions in that quarter to derive the quarter-end. Example: if a quarter ends with £500 and there were ten outflow transactions of £20 each (£200 total) during that quarter, the previous quarter ended with £700. Reverse the sign for inflows.

Mark each derived snapshot with `row_confidence`: 0.95+ for explicit values, 0.75–0.95 for derived, below 0.75 for gappy.

Set `derived: false` ONLY for quarter-ends the document states explicitly. Every snapshot you computed from transactions or neighbouring quarters is `derived: true`, even if `row_confidence` is high.
