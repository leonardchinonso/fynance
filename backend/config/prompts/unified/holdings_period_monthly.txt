HOLDINGS PERIOD: monthly
For every month covered by the documents, emit an end-of-month snapshot per distinct holding (`as_of` = last calendar day of that month).

If the documents only give one snapshot in a month, work forwards or backwards using the transactions in that month to derive the other month-ends. Example: if the month ends with £50 and there were five outflow transactions of £5 each (£25 total) during that month, the previous month ended with £75. Reverse the sign for inflows.

Mark each derived snapshot with `row_confidence` reflecting how directly the value was supported by the data: 0.95+ when the document gave the balance explicitly, 0.75–0.95 when you derived it from neighbour months and complete transactions, below 0.75 when the chain has gaps.
